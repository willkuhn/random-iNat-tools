Smokies ATBI new records from iNaturalist

Tracks iNaturalist observations that document taxa new to the checklists of Great Smoky Mountains National Park's All Taxa Biodiversity Inventory (ATBI). Observations are tagged and tracked with the observation field New Smokies ATBI record?. Counts include observations inside the park (iNaturalist place 72645); select any number to see those observations on iNaturalist.

How the workflow works

  1. Watch. We look for verifiable iNaturalist observations from inside the park of interesting or unusual taxa that may be new to the park.
  2. Check the ID. Do the photos or sounds support an identification at that rank? We verify it ourselves or ask experts to weigh in. While we wait for that help, the observation is marked awaiting ID confirmation.
  3. Check the checklists. Once the ID is solid, we compare the taxon and its synonyms against the park's species checklists. If it's missing, the observation is marked with the highest rank it would add, like species (confirming) when the park already has the genus but not the species.
  4. Park confirmation. Park staff double-check the observation and search the park's checklists and collections databases. If they agree it's new, they add it to the park checklist and the value changes to the plain rank (species), with a comment on the observation noting the new record. If a record is later re-identified as something already known, the field is removed and a comment explains why.
